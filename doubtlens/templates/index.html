Capabilities

Everything a student needs. Nothing they don't.

01

Reads photos and handwriting

Multimodal vision extracts equations, diagrams, and notes from textbook snapshots.

02

English and Hindi

Learn in the language you think in. Full native Devanagari script explanations and terminology.

03

Step-by-step explanations

No skipped algebra or mysterious leaps. Every derivation and theorem broken down sequentially.

04

Instant quiz with reasons

5 targeted multiple-choice questions per topic with conceptual explanations for each answer.

Built on open weights.
